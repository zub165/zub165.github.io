Common topics

  • Sign in / password — Use "Forgot password" on the login screen. You can also change your password in Settings after signing in.
  • Appointments — Book from the "Book appointment" screen or open Appointments to view upcoming visits.
  • Available doctors — After signing in, open Discovery or Book appointment to see providers on the platform.
  • Doctor registration — Licensed clinicians can apply in the app under Become a Provider.
  • Subscriptions / billing — Manage your plan in the Client hub. Subscriptions are managed through your App Store account.
  • Medical records — Available after sign-in under Medical records.
  • Hospitals map — Allow location access when prompted to see nearby hospitals.

Delete account

You can delete your account from within the app: Settings → Delete account. For help, see account deletion instructions.

Report a problem

Include your device model, iOS version, app version, and steps to reproduce the issue. Screenshots are helpful. You can also submit in-app feedback from the Feedback tab when signed in.
