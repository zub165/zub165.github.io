Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to Islamic Ambience Generator ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and web service.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Audio recordings you create and save
  • Settings and preferences you configure
  • Feedback and support requests

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device information (model, operating system, version)
  • Usage data (features used, session duration)
  • Crash reports and error logs

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain our service
  • To improve and personalize your experience
  • To analyze usage patterns and fix bugs
  • To respond to your support requests

4. Data Storage

All audio recordings and settings are stored locally on your device. We do not upload your recordings to our servers unless you explicitly choose to share them.

5. Permissions

Our app may request the following permissions:

  • Microphone: Required for recording audio sessions
  • Storage: Required for saving recordings and settings
  • Audio: Required for playing background music

6. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services for analytics and crash reporting. These services may collect anonymous usage data to help us improve the app.

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your stored data
  • Delete your recordings and data
  • Export your recordings
  • Opt-out of analytics (if applicable)

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
