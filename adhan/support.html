How to use

  1. Allow location so prayer times can be calculated for your city
  2. Review today’s times on the home screen
  3. Use Settings to choose adhan audio, calculation method, theme, and language
  4. Open Qibla compass from the compass icon

No sign-in is required. For store reviewers: open the app, allow or skip location, open Settings, and play an adhan from a prayer card.
