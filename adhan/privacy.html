What we collect

Adhan App calculates prayer times on your device. Location is used only to compute prayer times and Qibla. Preferences (adhan choice, theme, language, alarms) are stored locally on your device.

The app may request prayer times from the public Aladhan HTTPS API using latitude and longitude. Those requests do not include your name or account.

We do not require an account. We do not show ads or sell data.

How we use data

Local data is used only for prayer times, notifications, and app settings. Location is not shared with Innovators Generation servers.
