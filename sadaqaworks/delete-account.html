Delete your Sadaqaworks account

Preferred method: use Settings → Delete account & data in the mobile app. This clears local data and requests server deletion when you are signed in.

In the app (recommended)

  1. Open Sadaqaworks on Android or iOS.
  2. Go to the Settings tab.
  3. Under Legal & Support, tap Delete account & data.
  4. Confirm. Local app data is cleared, your session ends, and a delete request is sent to the server when online.

Email request

If you cannot open the app, email zub165@yahoo.com from the address linked to your account with subject Sadaqaworks account deletion. We process requests within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Local cached wallet, Zakat, contractor, and beneficiary data on your device
  • Server account via DELETE https://api.mywaitime.com/api/users/me/ when authenticated
  • Zakat ecosystem records associated with your email may be anonymized or removed on request
