1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide AI-assisted symptom triage, chat assessments, and HOPI-style note generation. We do not sell your personal information.

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Health-related inputs: Symptoms, medical questions, and clinical details you type or dictate into Chat or HOPI features.
  • Generated content: AI-generated triage summaries, assessments, and HOPI-style notes created from your inputs.
  • Voice input (optional): When you use speech-to-text, your device’s speech recognition may process audio to produce text. We do not intend to store raw audio on our servers unless required to provide the feature.
  • Content reports (optional): If you flag AI content, you may submit the message text, a reason, and optional details.
  • Account deletion requests (optional): If you use in-app account deletion, you may provide an email address and reason; this is used to process your request.
  • Subscription status: We receive subscription/purchase status from Apple App Store and Google Play (we do not receive your full payment card number).

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Session identifier: A random session_id (for example, direct-…) sent with chat and HOPI requests so conversations stay in context on our servers.
  • Usage data: Feature usage counts (for example, monthly HOPI/chat limits) stored on your device and used to enforce plan limits.
  • App settings: Language preference, theme, and configuration stored locally on your device.
  • Device/app metadata: Basic technical information needed to operate the service (for example, app version, platform).

1.3 What We Do Not Collect (current app version)

  • We do not require a user account for core chat/HOPI features.
  • We do not collect precise GPS location in the app.
  • We do not use third-party advertising SDKs or sell data for advertising in the current app version.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, or HealthKit/clinical records APIs unless a future version explicitly requests permission.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide quick triage, follow-up assessments, and HOPI note generation

5. Third-Party AI Service (OpenAI)

Medical HOPI Assistant uses OpenAI as a third-party AI service provider. When you use Chat, Quick Triage, or HOPI features, your data is sent to our backend server (encrypted HTTPS), which forwards it to OpenAI (GPT-4o API) to generate AI responses.

Data sent to our server and OpenAI may include:

  • Your message text (symptoms, questions, or patient/clinical information you type or dictate)
  • A session identifier (session_id) to keep conversation context
  • Your selected language code (e.g., en, ur, ar)
  • Recent conversation history for context (where applicable)

Who receives this data:

  • Our backend server — session management, medical knowledge retrieval (RAG), and routing to OpenAI
  • OpenAI, LLC — AI text generation via the OpenAI API (OpenAI Privacy Policy)

Your permission (required): Before any data is sent, the app shows a “Permission to Use AI” dialog that lists what is sent, who receives it, and links to this policy. You must tap Agree & Continue before the first AI request. You may tap Not now to decline. You can revoke consent anytime in Settings → Legal & Privacy → Data Sharing Consent.

OpenAI data protection: Data submitted through the OpenAI API is not used by OpenAI to train or improve their models, per OpenAI’s API data usage policies. We do not sell your data or share it for third-party advertising.
